# Logos

The Pointer mark: four stars joined by a dashed sightline that leads to one amber guide star, inside an orbit ring. It says what the work does, which is pointing people to the sky.

- `pointer-mark-night.svg`: on `void`, `night` or photos darker than 30% luminance. Ring and line `dim`, stars `star`, guide star `amber`.
- `pointer-mark-day.svg`: on `void` in the Day theme and on paper. Ring and line `dim` (Day), stars `star` (Day), guide star `amber` (Day).
- `app-icon.svg`: favicon, social avatar and app icon. The night mark on a `void` rounded square.

Clear space: keep the width of the ring's radius free on every side. Minimum size: 20px on screen, 8mm in print. Below that, use the guide star alone as a single amber dot.

Do not recolor the guide star, rotate the mark, add glows, or place the night mark on light grounds.
