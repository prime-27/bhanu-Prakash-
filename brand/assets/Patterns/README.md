# Patterns

Background textures for slides, social posts, certificates and printed material, where the live particle universe cannot run.

- `starfield.svg`: a seeded field of 170 stars in the particle palette (`particle-warm`, `particle-cool`, `particle-ember`, `star`, with the brightest few in `amber`) on `void`. Use it full-bleed behind titles; keep text on the left, where stars are sparse.
- `orbits.svg`: five orbit arcs at `dim` strength with four planets, on `night`. Use it for section dividers and cover pages.

Both are tile-free stills of the website's motion. Never animate them in place of the real particle scenes.
